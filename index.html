5 Golden Rules for Safe Buying

1

Check the Final Total (Not Just Item Price)

2

Protect Your Personal & Card Info

Never type real passwords, home addresses, or real bank cards on practice or unfamiliar websites.

3

Watch Out for Shipping Fees

Many stores offer free shipping once your cart reaches a certain amount (like $25 here!).

4

Double-Check Quantities

Make sure you didn't accidentally add 2 or 3 of the same item before placing your order.

Smart UK Online Shopping Tips

4 Golden Rules for UK Shoppers

1

Look for HTTPS & the Padlock Icon

Before entering personal details on real websites, check the address bar for https:// and a padlock symbol so your data is encrypted.

2

Know Your UK Consumer Rights (14-Day Returns)

Under the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations, you usually have 14 days to cancel and return most online orders after delivery.

3

Check VAT & Delivery Charges

UK retail prices normally include 20% VAT, but delivery charges are often added at checkout unless you hit the free delivery threshold (like £20 here).

4

Never Use Real Bank Cards on Practice Sites

Keep your real debit card details, sort code, and home address private when practising or browsing unfamiliar sites.
